Amazon Web Services (AWS) is a subsidiary of Amazon that provides cloud computing platforms.
Jeff Bezos founded Amazon in 1994 in Seattle, Washington.

Amazon operates the largest e-commerce platform in the United States.
AWS was launched in 2006 and has become the leading cloud provider.

Andy Jassy became CEO of Amazon in 2021, succeeding Jeff Bezos.
Amazon Prime is a subscription service with over 200 million members worldwide.

The company's headquarters, known as the Amazon Spheres, is located in Seattle.
Amazon competes with Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud Platform in the cloud computing market.
