Apple Inc. is a multinational technology company headquartered in Cupertino, California. 
The company was founded by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne in 1976.

Apple's most successful product is the iPhone, which was introduced by Steve Jobs in 2007.
Tim Cook became CEO of Apple in 2011, succeeding Steve Jobs.

The company also produces the MacBook, iPad, and Apple Watch.
Apple's headquarters is located at Apple Park in Cupertino.

In 2023, Apple reached a market capitalization of over 3 trillion dollars.
Microsoft and Google are major competitors in the technology sector.
